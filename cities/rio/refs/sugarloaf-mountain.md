# Sugarloaf Mountain (Pão de Açúcar)

Granite and quartz monolith at the mouth of Guanabara Bay. Wikidata [Q210722](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q210722).
`landmark.py` could not fetch this card (Wikidata answered 429 during the run and the cached reply has no entity), so
these are general facts written by hand, not fetched data.

| fact | value |
|---|---|
| height | ~396 m (Morro da Urca beside it ~220 m) |
| material | granite, bare on the steep faces, scrub and forest on the shoulders |
| cable car | opened 1912; two spans: Praia Vermelha station → Morro da Urca → Sugarloaf summit; two cars per span shuttle in opposite directions |

Drawn at 40 u to the summit (Urca at 22 u): heavily compressed, as every landmark here.
